About Me

The longer version — and where to find me around the web.

I’m a marketer, cyclist, and serial tinkerer. I live in Jersey City Heights with a growing collection of handmade furniture and a coffee setup that’s fussier than it needs to be. Originally from Pittsburgh.

When I’m not building something, I’m biking the Empire State Trail, writing with stationery, drawing stationary objects, and reading non-fiction books.

An extremely nerdy joke I told on Bluesky once made Ben Thompson laugh out loud on an episode of Dithering.

I used to have my own clothing company. It was called bminus.
